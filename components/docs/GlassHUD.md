# 🚀 RK3 Glass HUD Laws (Locked for Eternity)

This document defines the permanent design laws for all cockpit, dashboard, and portal UI under RK3.  
These laws are non-negotiable and enforce the **WOW factor**. Every future feature must pass the **Glass HUD vibe check**.

---

## 1. Transparency First
- All cockpit + dashboard panels must be **glassmorphic**:
  - `bg-white/5` or resonance-tinted glass (`bg-emerald-400/10`, etc).
  - `backdrop-blur-md` to refract starfield behind it.
  - `border border-white/20` (or resonance border).
  - `shadow-xl` for depth and holographic glow.

---

## 2. Cosmic Visibility
- Background **starfield, shooting stars, and orbs** must always be visible *through* HUD layers.
- No solid blocks. Nothing hides the cosmos.

## 4. Energy Feedback

  - Panel SFX (touch/press) on HUD interactions.
  - HUD glow pulse when **Now Playing** updates.

---

## 5. Living HUD
- HUD is never static:
  - Subtle **breathing animation** (scale/opacity cycles).
  - Sparkles / stardust in **Now Playing ticker**.
  - Shooting stars + orbs drift across cockpit always.

---

## 6. Futuristic Control
- Cockpit HUDs must feel like **Minority Report hand-swipes**:
  - Floating, movable panels (future upgrade: draggable/rearrangeable).
  - Smooth `framer-motion` transitions.
  - Cinematic, not flat UI.
